q: What is ALAT?
a: ALAT by Wema is Wema Bank’s fully Digital Banking Platform that allows you conduct all your banking transactions without ever having to visit any physical branch.
q: Who can use ALAT?
a: Everyone! you simply need to get registered on a smartphone. To operate your account, you may use your smartphone or revert to the *945# USSD banking.
q: How much does it cost to get ALAT?
a: ALAT is free to sign up on.
q: Which mobile platforms are supported by the ALAT Application?
a: The ALAT is currently supported on all IOS and Android devices.  Please visit your Google Play store or App Store to download the application.
q: Can I create goals on ALAT?
a: Yes, goals creation is allowed for all tier three savings and current account holders on ALAT.
