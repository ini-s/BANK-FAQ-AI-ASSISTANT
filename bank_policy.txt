1. Whistle-Blowing Policy

Wema Bank encourages transparency and integrity through its whistle-blowing policy. Employees, customers, and third parties are allowed to report any misconduct, malpractice, or regulatory violations confidentially. The policy ensures that whistle-blowers are protected from retaliation, and any confirmed reports of wrongdoing may result in disciplinary action, including dismissal or legal prosecution.

2. Customer Due Diligence (CDD) / Know Your Customer (KYC) Policy

The bank requires proper identification and verification of customers before establishing any banking relationship. This process helps prevent fraud, money laundering, and terrorism financing. High-risk customers, such as politically exposed persons (PEPs), undergo enhanced due diligence and must receive approval from senior management before their accounts are opened or maintained.

3. Sustainability and Responsible Banking Policy

Wema Bank integrates sustainability into its operations and decision-making. It aligns its business strategy with global sustainable development goals and promotes environmentally and socially responsible banking practices. The bank supports initiatives such as environmental protection, financial literacy, and women empowerment as part of its long-term corporate responsibility.

4. Financial Inclusion and Access Policy

The bank is committed to increasing financial inclusion by providing accessible products and services for unbanked and underbanked populations. It develops simple and affordable account options, leverages digital channels, and promotes mobile and agent banking to make financial services available to everyone, especially in underserved areas.

5. Capital Adequacy and Regulatory Compliance Policy

Wema Bank maintains a strong capital base in line with the Central Bank of Nigeria’s requirements for national banks. It regularly reviews its capital adequacy to ensure stability, solvency, and full compliance with regulatory standards. The policy reinforces prudent financial management and promotes confidence among customers and stakeholders.

6. Privacy and Data Protection Policy

The bank respects customers’ privacy and safeguards all personal and financial information in compliance with data protection regulations. It collects and processes customer data solely for legitimate banking purposes and ensures that such data is securely stored, used responsibly, and not shared without consent or legal justification.

7. Fraud Management Policy

Wema Bank adopts proactive measures to prevent, detect, and respond to fraudulent activities. This includes continuous staff training, transaction monitoring, customer education, and collaboration with law enforcement agencies to mitigate fraud risk.

8. Sanctions Compliance Policy

The bank complies with all applicable local and international sanctions regulations. It ensures that no banking relationship or transaction violates economic, trade, or financial sanctions issued by regulatory authorities.

9. Anti-Bribery and Corruption Policy

Wema Bank maintains a zero-tolerance stance toward bribery and corruption. All employees, agents, and partners are required to act with integrity and must not offer, solicit, or accept bribes in any form. The bank enforces disciplinary measures for breaches of this policy.

10. Service Management Policy

The bank is committed to delivering high-quality, reliable, and customer-focused banking services. It continuously reviews its processes, service standards, and customer feedback to ensure operational efficiency and client satisfaction.

11. Cybercrime Prohibition and Prevention Policy

Wema Bank enforces strict cybersecurity controls to protect customers’ digital assets and personal data. The policy aligns with Nigeria’s Cybercrime (Prohibition, Prevention, etc.) Act and focuses on preventing unauthorized access, online fraud, data breaches, and other cyber threats through secure systems and user awareness.
